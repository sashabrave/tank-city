One large square sheet with a clean 3x3 grid of 9 different MINIMALIST game logo variations for the video game "WAR CATS". Thin even gutters, each cell on a plain flat dark charcoal background, no labels, no numbers, no extra text anywhere.

Keep it simple and clean — fewer details, bold shapes, lots of breathing room, strong readable silhouette that works small as an app icon or title.

Every logo follows the same brief:
- The title "WAR CATS" in ALL CAPS, in the Rubik Dirt font style: heavy rounded bold letters with a light distressed edge. The letters are made of bricks, shown simply: a few clean mortar lines in flat orange-red brick colour, one simple bevel, one dark outline. No busy texture. Spelling exactly W-A-R C-A-T-S.
- To the RIGHT of the letters, a small chunky simplified olive-green tank driving to the RIGHT, cannon pointing right. In the hatch sits the cat EXACTLY like the cat reference image: orange tabby with big brown eyes, cream muzzle, pink nose, wearing an OLIVE-GREEN combat helmet with orange cat ears poking through, black strap and orange goggles pushed up on the helmet, olive uniform with an orange armband. Not a white helmet.
- A handful of clean brick chunks fly off the letters (5–10 pieces, not a cloud of debris), a couple of simple speed lines, maybe one small puff of dust — dynamic and powerful but minimal.
- Style: flat matte low-poly clay 3D, very limited palette: brick orange, olive green, cream, charcoal. Simple soft lighting.

Make the 9 cells different from each other: one line vs two stacked lines, slight tilt, brick colour shade, number and direction of flying bricks, tank pose (straight, slight wheelie, jumping), size balance between title and tank. Every cell is a complete finished logo with the full title readable.
